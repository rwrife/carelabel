Keep your clothing care instructions close at hand with Care Label. Build a personal garment registry and record the details you need before laundry day.

YOUR GARMENTS, ORGANIZED
Add garments by name and category, include a fabric note, and search your collection when you need to find an item. Edit records as your wardrobe changes.

CARE DETAILS IN ONE PLACE
Record washing temperature and cycle, bleaching, drying, ironing, and professional-care instructions. Missing information stays clearly marked so you know when to check the original label.

UNDERSTAND THE SYMBOLS
Open the built-in symbol reference for plain-language explanations of clothing care symbols while entering a garment's instructions.

KEEP THE ORIGINAL LABEL HANDY
Attach a label photo from your photo library and review your recorded care rules from the garment's detail screen.

Care Label helps you organize the information on your garments. Always follow the manufacturer's label and instructions.
